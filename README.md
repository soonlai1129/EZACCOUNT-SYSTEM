The ezAccount System is a simple and practical tool designed to help business owners
manage their finances more easily and accurately. Instead of relying on Microsoft Excel or
manually writing down transactions, which can be time-consuming and prone to errors, this
system automatically records income, expenses, and other financial activities in an organized
way. It stores everything in one place, making it easier to track and access past records
whenever needed. The system can also generate useful reports such as monthly summaries
and profit and loss statements, helping users better understand their business performance.
With ezAccount System, business owners can stay organized, save time, and focus more on
growing their business rather than dealing with paperwork and manual calculations.


INTRODUCTION

Nowadays, many small business owners still use tools like Microsoft Excel or Word
to calculate profits, track sales, and manage their daily spending. These tools are easy to start
with and quite common, but as the business gets bigger, the work can become messy,
confusing, and take up too much time. That’s why our group decided to build ezAccount
System, it is a simple and friendly accounting system made specially for small businesses.
Our goal is to make calculations easier, reduce mistakes, and save time so business owners
can focus on what really matters, that’s growing their business.
ezAccount System is designed to be affordable and easy to use, even for those
without any accounting experience. It helps track income and expenses, and create clear
summaries and reports. The system can also automatically show how the business is doing
on a weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly basis, making it easier to spot trends and make
better decisions. With ezAccount System, we want to take the stress out of managing
business finances and offer a smoother, more comfortable experience for every small
business owner.


1.1 BACKGROUND OF PROJECT

In recent years, many small businesses have continued to rely on basic tools like
Microsoft Excel and Word to handle their financial tasks such as calculating profits, tracking
sales, and managing daily expenses. While these tools are widely used and easy to access,
they are not designed for accounting purposes. As a business grows, using these tools can
become inefficient, confusing, and prone to errors, especially for those without an
accounting background.
To address this problem, our group is developing ezAccount System, it is a simple,
user-friendly, and affordable accounting solution tailored specifically for small businesses.
This system aims to help business owners handle their finances more effectively, without
the need for complex software or professional accounting knowledge. ezAccount System
provides features that allow users to track income and expenses, generate clear financial
summaries, and view business performance automatically on a weekly, monthly, quarterly,
and yearly basis.
By providing a more organized and comfortable way to manage finances, ezAccount
System supports small business owners in making smarter decisions, saving time, and
focusing more on running and growing their business.

1.3 OBJECTIVES

To develop ezAccount System, a user-friendly and affordable accounting tool that helps
small business owners manage their finances more efficiently and gain better insights into
their business performance. In fact, the objectives of ezAccount System are:

  i. To make it easier for small business owners to move away from Microsoft Excel or
      Word by offering a simple system that helps them track their finances in one place.

  ii. To save time and avoid common calculation mistakes by letting the system handle
      things like adding up income, tracking expenses, and generating reports
      automatically.
  
  iii. To help business owners clearly understand how their business is doing by showing
        easy-to-read reports every week, month, quarter, and year.


1.4 SCOPE

1.4.1 System Scope

  i. Business Owner:
    • Add and manage supplier details, item descriptions, prices, and stock
      quantities.
    • View and search all past transaction records.
    • Generate financial summaries on a monthly, quarterly, or yearly basis.
    • Create and manage sales and transaction records for business tracking.
    
ii. Staff:
    • Assist the business owner by managing stock entries when the business
    owner is not available.
    • Add and update stock details such as item names, prices, and quantities.
    • Help ensure stock records are always accurate and up to date.

    ii. Staff


1.5 PROJECT SIGNIFICANT/BENEFIT

The ezAccount system is designed to make managing business finances simple and stressfree by replacing manual tools like Excel or Word. It saves time, reduces errors, and
automates financial tasks and calculations, so owners can focus on running their business
instead of struggling with numbers. With clear, easy-to-understand reports, business owners
can track performance and make smarter decisions with confidence. Real-time data adds
transparency and keeps them in control of their finances at all times. Plus, the system is built
to grow with the business, offering scalable features that adapt as needs change.
The ezAccount system also provides flexibility and accessibility, making it suitable for small
business owners with different levels of technical or financial knowledge. Its user-friendly
design ensures that even those without an accounting background can easily navigate the
system, reducing the learning curve and eliminating the need for costly training. By
integrating essential features like expense tracking, closing sales, stock updates, and profit
and loss statement into one centralized platform, it helps business owners keep everything
organized in a seamless way. This not only improves day-to-day efficiency but also builds
confidence in financial decision-making, giving small businesses a strong foundation to
grow sustainably.
